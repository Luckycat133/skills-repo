# Support & Community

Welcome to the **Agent Context Migrator** community! Here is how you can get help, report issues, contribute, or sponsor development.

---

## 💬 Community Support & Discussions

We use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Luckycat133/skills-repo/discussions) for open collaboration:

- **💡 Ideas & Feedback**: Share suggestions for improving migration workflows, CLI ergonomics, or ACB features.
- **🛠️ Show & Tell**: Share successful migration stories, device setups, or tips for moving between AI coding agents.
- **🚀 IDE Support Requests**: Discuss and vote on upcoming AI coding agents and IDE profiles.
- **❓ Q&A**: Ask questions about specific adapters, MCP conversions, or multi-device setups.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Path Drift

- **Found a bug?** Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/Luckycat133/skills-repo/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **IDE configuration path changed?** Help us keep the registry fresh by submitting a [Path Update](https://github.com/Luckycat133/skills-repo/issues/new?template=verify_path.yml).
